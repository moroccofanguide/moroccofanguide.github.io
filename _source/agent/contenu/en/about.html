Morocco Fan Guide is an independent, practical guide to visiting Morocco and preparing for the 2030 World Cup. It is run by a Morocco resident who lives in Agadir.

What you will find here

Concrete advice to travel without bad surprises: getting around, knowing real prices, avoiding scams, choosing day trips. Articles are published in French and English.

How the articles are written

  • Local experience. Benchmarks on taxis, prices, safety and daily life come from the author's experience in Morocco. Prices are orders of magnitude and vary by city, neighbourhood and season.
  • Fact-checking. Practical information is checked against official sources where they exist: UK and French government travel advice, Moroccan authorities, UNESCO, transport companies.
  • Use of artificial intelligence. An AI assistant helps with research, structure and translation. Every article is then reviewed and its facts checked before publication. Reviews, prices and anecdotes are never made up.
  • Updates. Each article shows its last update date. Outdated information is corrected or removed.

How the site is funded

The site is free. Some booking links (activities, accommodation, transport) are partner links: if you book through them, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. These links do not influence the advice given.
