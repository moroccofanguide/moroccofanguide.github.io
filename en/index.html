Getting to Morocco, getting around, enjoying the cities and preparing for the 2030 World Cup: practical advice, written from Morocco.

Where to start

Getting there

Taking the ferry

From Spain (Algeciras, Tarifa, Almería, Motril) or France (notably Sète), ferries sail to Tanger Med, Tanger Ville and Nador. Crossing the Strait takes roughly one to two hours; in summer, book early.

On the ground

Things to do in the main cities

Medinas, souks, riads, desert and Atlas trips: our activity ideas for Marrakech, Fes, Tangier, Rabat, Casablanca and Agadir.

2030

The 2030 World Cup in Morocco

Morocco co-hosts the tournament with Spain and Portugal. Cities, stadiums, transport, accommodation: we will follow the official announcements to help you plan your trip.

Cities to watch for 2030

  • Casablanca: the country's main air gateway and business capital
  • Rabat: the capital, one hour from Casablanca by train
  • Tangier: a short hop from Spain, linked to Rabat and Casablanca by the Al Boraq high-speed train
  • Marrakech: the most visited city in the country
  • Fes: one of the oldest medinas in the world, at the heart of imperial Morocco
  • Agadir: the Atlantic beach resort

The final list of stadiums and the match schedule will be confirmed by the organisers; we will update this guide with every announcement.
