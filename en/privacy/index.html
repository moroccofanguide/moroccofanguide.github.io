Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026.

What the site collects

Morocco Fan Guide has no user accounts, forms or newsletter. The site itself does not ask you for any personal data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may process technical data, such as your IP address, in its server logs to deliver and protect the site.

Partner links and cookies

The site uses a script from our affiliate partner Travelpayouts, which turns some booking links (GetYourGuide, Booking.com and others) into partner links. This script and the partner sites may set cookies in your browser to attribute a booking to the site. Their processing is covered by the privacy policies of Travelpayouts and the partner sites.

You can refuse or delete these cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Social networks

Pages contain a Pinterest verification tag, which sets no cookie. There are no share buttons loading social network scripts.

Audience measurement

Since 22 October 2026 the site uses Ahrefs Web Analytics to count visits. According to Ahrefs, it sets no cookie, does not store IP addresses and does not track a visitor from one day to the next. It records the pages viewed, the referring site, your country, your browser and your device type.
