## Age, licence, who can ride

Quad rides in Agafay are designed for beginners: several listings say no licence is required, the quads are automatic and a guide explains the controls before departure. The minimum riding age is often 16, with a signed parental authorisation for 16 and 17-year-olds, but at least one operator asks for 18. Younger children can sometimes ride as passengers behind an adult (from 5 or 6 depending on the listing): ask for the exact rule before booking.

Several listings advise against the activity for pregnant women and for people with back or heart problems, because of the bumpy terrain. Weight limits per quad or per group also exist, and they differ from one operator to another.

## Duration: from 40 minutes to a full day

This is the biggest difference between offers. A "quad ride" can mean a short loop of a few dozen minutes added to a dinner, two hours of riding in a small group, or a whole day with lunch. Allow about 45 minutes each way between Marrakech and the desert, plus a short safety briefing. If you really want to ride, check the riding time written in the listing, not just the total length of the excursion.

## What to wear and practical tips

- **Clothes:** long trousers and a breathable long-sleeved top that you don't mind getting dusty.
- **Closed shoes**, never flip-flops or sandals.
- **Protection:** a scarf over nose and mouth, sunglasses (if not provided), sunscreen, a hat.
- **A jacket for the evening:** temperatures drop after sunset, even in summer.
- **Water and ID:** some operators ask for a document before handing over the quad.

**Tip:** sunset is the most requested slot. Book ahead in high season, and ask whether the quad is included or an extra in a dinner-show package.

## Choosing an operator

- **Real riding time** and the number of quads in the group: a small group is nicer than a convoy.
- **Transfer included** from your hotel or riad, and the return time.
- **Insurance and excess** in case of damage or a fall: ask what is covered before paying, as listings say little.
- **Equipment:** a clean helmet that fits, goggles, the condition of the quads.
- **Price:** it varies widely with duration, group and inclusions (transfer, tea, dinner). Compare like with like rather than a headline price; we give no figures here, for lack of a reliable, current source.
- **Free cancellation** and recent reviews.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you need a licence to ride a quad in Agafay?

According to the operator listings we read, no: the quads are automatic and a guide explains the controls before departure. Bring an ID anyway.

### What is the minimum age for quad biking in Agafay?

Often 16 with parental consent, but some operators require 18. Younger children can sometimes ride as passengers. Check the rule on your booking.

### How long does a quad ride in Agafay last?

Riding time ranges from under an hour to about 2 hours depending on the package. With the transfer from Marrakech, allow about 4.5 to 5 hours for a half day.

### What should you wear for quad biking in the desert?

Long trousers, a light long-sleeved top, closed shoes, a scarf and sunglasses against the dust, and a jacket for the evening.

### Quad or buggy: which one?

This article focuses on quad biking, the only vehicle for which we have recent traveller accounts. For a buggy, compare each offer's conditions (age, seats, duration) before choosing.

### Is quad biking possible in summer?

Yes, but it gets very hot during the day: prefer late afternoon and bring water.
